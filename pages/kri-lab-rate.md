# G3+ Lab Abnormality Rate

## Steps to Perform the Review

1. In the KRI Report by Site and Country, toggle to the G3+ Lab Abnormality Rate results.

2. Identify outliers.

### For High Outliers

This indicates a potential safety risk or a data issue such as incorrect units causing the result to appear >= Grade 3. Consider the overall number of samples in the numerator and number of subjects impacted. If the number of samples is very low and is only impacting a single subject, escalation may not be warranted.

### For Low Outliers

Consider if the site may be missing lab sample collections and thus have fewer samples making it less likely that G3+ abnormalities are observed. This could also indicate a less sick patient population for the site. Generally, low outliers in this KRI are unexpected.
