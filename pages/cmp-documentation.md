# CMP & Documentation

## Central Monitoring Plan

Typically, Central Monitoring details for a given study are documented in the study-specific Central Monitoring Plan (CMP) including analyses to be used, frequency of review, and other pertinent details.

## Documenting CM Review

Typically, CM processes should include documentation of all the signals surfaced by analytics tools, the decision to action a signal or not (with rationale), and documentation of the review cycle (to align with the study-specific CMP).
